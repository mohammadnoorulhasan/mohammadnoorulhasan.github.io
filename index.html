About me

The part of ML that happens after the model works

I'm an MLOps engineer, currently a Senior AI Engineer at Best Buy India. My work starts where a data scientist's notebook ends: turning models into automated pipelines that ingest, preprocess, deploy and make decisions on their own, then supporting them in production.

Before MLOps I built the models myself, across document intelligence, AML screening, process automation and computer vision. That background makes me a better partner to data science teams, because I know what they need from a platform.

I also teach system design and AI on my YouTube channel, The AI & Systems Lab HQ.
