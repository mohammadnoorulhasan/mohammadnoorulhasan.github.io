About me

From computer vision models to production ML platforms

I'm Mohammad Noor Ul Hasan, a Senior AI Engineer at Best Buy in Bengaluru. Over seven years I've worked across the whole AI lifecycle: data, models, serving, and running systems reliably in production.

I started in computer vision at TCS with license plate recognition, then built insurance AI at Future Generali: video KYC liveness detection, Aadhaar masking and AML screening.

Since 2023 I've worked on Best Buy's AI, first through Wipro and now directly: a deals recommender serving 1,000 requests per second on GKE, Gemini-based classification of 50–60k support queries a day, and Vertex AI pipelines. I also teach system design and AI on my YouTube channel, The AI & Systems Lab HQ.

ML serving and MLOps

Real-time model APIs on GKE, Vertex AI pipelines, Cloud Run services and load testing for production readiness.

GenAI, search and recommendations

Gemini-based classification, vector search, feature stores, learning-to-rank evaluation and Redis-backed search.

Computer vision and document AI

Liveness detection, PII masking and license plate recognition with PyTorch, Keras, YOLO and OCR.
