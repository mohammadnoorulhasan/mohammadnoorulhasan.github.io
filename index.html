Featured case study · Best Buy

LLM classification for customer support

Free-text customer queries arrive from support channels. Gemini extracts intent, brand, product and damage type from each one, and a self-hosted small language model stands by as the fallback.

50–60kqueries a day
5 RPSper L4 GPU, SLM backup
Key decision

A hosted LLM as the primary model, with an SLM served on vLLM as a self-hosted backup, so classification keeps running if the primary is unavailable.
