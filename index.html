About me

From computer vision models to production ML platforms

I'm Mohammad Noor Ul Hasan, a Senior AI Engineer at Best Buy in Bengaluru. Over seven years I've worked across the whole AI lifecycle: preparing data, training models, integrating them into applications, and running them in production.

I began with computer vision and OCR at TCS, building license plate recognition and driver face authentication. At Future Generali I shipped document AI and fraud-prevention systems for insurance: Aadhaar masking, document classification, video liveness detection and AML screening.

Since 2023 I've focused on MLOps on Google Cloud, first at Wipro for Best Buy and now at Best Buy directly: Vertex AI pipelines that take data science models into reliable, repeatable production. I also teach system design and AI on my YouTube channel, The AI & Systems Lab HQ.

MLOps on Google Cloud

Vertex AI Pipelines and BigQuery workflows for data preparation, inference and results, built from reusable components.

Computer vision and OCR

Detection, classification, face liveness and document understanding with YOLO, VGG19, DeepPixBis and OCR.

NLP and search

Text classification, fuzzy name and address matching for screening, and faster product search with Redis.
